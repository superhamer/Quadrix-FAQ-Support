# Quadrix — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Quadrix?**
Quadrix is a premium task prioritization app built around the Eisenhower Matrix methodology. It helps you cut through the noise and focus on what truly matters by intelligently categorizing your tasks by urgency and importance.

**Who made Quadrix?**
Quadrix was developed by Maher Ladsak, CMA, CPA(PH) in collaboration with Rork.com. All rights reserved.

**What platforms does Quadrix support?**
Quadrix is available on iOS and Android.

**Is Quadrix free?**
Yes. Quadrix is free to download and use with no ads or in-app purchases.

---

## Getting Started

**How does the Eisenhower Matrix work in Quadrix?**
When you open the app, your tasks are organized into four priority quadrants. Each quadrant represents a different combination of urgency and importance, guiding you on what to act on immediately, what to schedule, what to delegate, and what to let go of.

**What's the best way to start using Quadrix?**
Begin by creating your first project and adding a few tasks. As you add tasks, place them into the quadrant that best reflects their priority. Over time, you'll develop an intuitive sense for categorization that makes your workflow faster and more effective.

---

## Projects

**What is a project?**
A project is a dedicated workspace for a specific area of your life or work. Each project has its own set of four priority quadrants. You might have separate projects for Work, Personal, Health, or any category that makes sense to you.

**Can I have multiple projects?**
Yes. Quadrix supports multiple projects with easy navigation between them. When you have more than one project, an automatically generated homepage provides a consolidated overview of all your tasks.

**Is there a limit to how many projects I can create?**
No hard limit is enforced.

---

## Tasks

**What can I do with tasks?**
Each task supports rich content including text, attachments (images, files up to 10 MB), voice recordings with playback, and scheduled reminders with push notifications.

**Can I move tasks between quadrants or projects?**
Yes. Tasks can be reassigned to different quadrants or transferred to other projects as your priorities evolve.

**Can I reorder tasks and projects?**
Yes. Both tasks within quadrants and projects in the project list support custom ordering.

**Is there an undo feature?**
A single-level undo is available for certain actions like deletions and transfers, giving you a safety net for accidental changes.

---

## Reminders & Calendar

**Does Quadrix support reminders?**
Yes. You can set date and time-based reminders on any task. The app delivers push notifications even when it's not actively open.

**Is there a calendar view?**
Yes. A dedicated calendar view shows all tasks with active scheduled reminders, giving you a timeline perspective on your upcoming commitments.

## Backup & Restore

**How does backup work?**
Quadrix offers multiple backup strategies:
- Export individual projects
- Create full system backups covering all projects
- Schedule automatic backups on a recurring basis (daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly)

Backups can be saved to external cloud services like iCloud Drive or Google Drive for safekeeping.

**How do I restore my data?**
You can restore from a previously created system backup or upload a backup file from your device.

> Restoring a backup will replace all existing data. This action cannot be undone.

**Important notes about backups:**
- Up to 3 cloud backups are retained, each available for a maximum of 6 months.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored backups. Always save a copy to an external location before uninstalling.
- Auto backups are stored locally and may be lost during a device reset. Manual external backups are strongly recommended.

## Print

**Can I print my tasks?**
Yes. Quadrix supports printing individual or multiple projects for offline reference.

## Recycle Bin

**What happens when I delete a task?**
Deleted tasks are moved to a Recycle Bin where they can be reviewed before permanent deletion. You can empty the Recycle Bin from Settings when you're ready to permanently remove all trashed items.
